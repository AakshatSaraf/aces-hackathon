Method & honest limits

Net(m,t) = Q·(1−shrink)·P̂ₘ(t+transit) − freight&fuel − toll − loading − 2% fee − storage·t, then a risk haircut: 70% expected + 30% P10-price blend, minus transit risk that grows with distance and rain.

Market Realization Score = risk-adjusted net ÷ (Q × highest modal price in any mandi today) × 100.

A farther option must beat local selling by 4% with non-low confidence, otherwise the safer local option wins. Road km = straight line × 1.3 (stand-in for OSRM). Prices, closures and rate cards are synthetic demo assumptions; plug in Agmarknet, Open-Meteo and OSRM for live use.
